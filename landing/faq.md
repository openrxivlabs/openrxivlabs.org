---
title: What is openRxiv Labs?
---

openRxiv Labs is an experimental space for pushing the boundaries of research communication on top of openRxiv’s corpus of preprints, figures, metadata, and other research outputs. We work with select partners to run bold, high-impact experiments that have the potential to advance the status quo of preprinting and open science, focused on a number of key opportunity areas selected in consultation with our community and stakeholders. To learn more about openRxiv Labs, and how to get involved, visit the openRxiv Labs website at https://openrxivlabs.org.
